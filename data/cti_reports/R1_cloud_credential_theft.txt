Analysts tracked a group known as CloudHarvest that searched compromised hosts for AWS credential files and collected access keys stored on disk (T1552.001). The harvested keys were then used to sign in to cloud accounts as if they were legitimate users (T1078.004). In one intrusion the operators listed storage buckets and downloaded objects from them (T1530). Command and control used 203.0.113.45 and update-check.example-c2.com.
