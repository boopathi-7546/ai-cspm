Architecture & Security Models

Transparent, deterministic, and verifiable. Understand how AI-CSPM evaluates posture and calculates risk.

Risk Fusion Formula

Raw risk combines static rule severity with asset criticality and CTI frequency:

Raw = Severity × AssetCriticality × (1 + CTI_weight)

CTI weight ∈ [0, 1] boosts findings up to 2× when confirmed in threat reports, without ever zeroing out non-CTI issues.

Supported Checks

Built-in deterministic rules covering key misconfigurations:

  • S3 Public Read & Unencrypted Buckets
  • IAM Administrator Wildcard Policies & Inactive Keys
  • Security Groups 0.0.0.0/0 SSH (22) & RDP (3389)
  • Unencrypted RDS Volumes & Open Endpoints
